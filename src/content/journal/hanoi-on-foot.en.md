We didn't hire a studio for our first lookbook. Instead, we walked.

## The walls

Hanoi has walls better than any backdrop: peeling yellow lime, mottled grey concrete, small patches of moss. Dark denim in front of them looks like it has always belonged there.

## The teal door

On an old street we found a teal iron door with curling ironwork and two blue-and-white ceramic pots on either side. We spent most of a morning there.

## Street-side coffee

The afternoon ended with an iced milk coffee on a low plastic stool in front of a graffiti wall. A good pair of trousers should be able to sit in places like that.
