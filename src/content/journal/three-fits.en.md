kokui's three fits share a starting point: a high rise and a leg wide enough to let the body relax. What changes is the path the leg takes from thigh to hem.

## Wide Straight

A wide leg that falls straight from the hip to the shoe. It is the most classic of the three, the trouser from old black-and-white photographs. It works with a shirt and tie in the morning and a black tee for an afternoon coffee.

## Barrel

Two curved side seams create a leg that is full at the knee and narrows at the hem. The Barrel has more character, especially with leather shoes and a simple belt.

## Tapered

Roomy through the hip and thigh, then gently narrowing. The Tapered is the neatest option, for anyone who wants the ease of a wide leg with a sharper finish.
